About Us

Nvidia's Journey

NVIDIA was founded on April 5, 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem. The company started with a bold vision: to bring 3D graphics and accelerated computing to personal computers. In its early days, NVIDIA focused on graphics chips for gaming, laying the foundation for what would later become a revolution in GPUs, AI, and high-performance computing.
